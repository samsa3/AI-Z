# 04 — Business case & demo

## Problem
_Who has it, how much time/money it costs today._

Starting point (recruiting example): HR teams spend hours reading applications and comparing them with job criteria. An AI agent could do this in minutes — but every prompt to an external model is a transfer of personal data to a processor, often outside the EU. Without controls: no legal basis, sensitive data leaked, no proof of compliance.

## Value of the agent
_Time saved, quality, scalability._

## Why our safeguards make it deployable
_A company can actually use it because…_

**Our pitch in one line:** AI can be useful *and* lawful — HR gets the productivity gain, candidates keep their rights, the company gets evidence it can show a regulator.

BRANE turns the law into enforceable rules: pseudonymise names, remove contact details, block health and other sensitive data, keep the re-identification key local, fail closed if the check is down, leave the final decision to a human — and log everything.

**AI Act timing argument:** recruiting AI is high-risk (Annex III). Prohibitions and AI literacy already apply; high-risk duties are deferred to Dec 2027 (Digital Omnibus — to verify), **not cancelled** → building them in now = compliance by design, no costly rebuild later. Deployers must also train staff (Art. 4), inform workers and candidates (Art. 26(7), 26(11)).

Compliance arguments (GDPR — https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj):
- **Risk avoided:** fines up to €20M or 4% of worldwide turnover for breaching principles, legal bases, Art. 9 or transfer rules (Art. 83(5)); up to €10M / 2% for missing privacy by design or security (Art. 83(4)); plus damages claims (Art. 82).
- **Privacy by design out of the box:** minimisation and pseudonymisation are built in, not added later (Art. 25).
- **Provable compliance:** logs + test protocol = the evidence the controller must be able to show (Art. 5(2), Art. 24, Art. 30).
- **Human stays in control:** the agent prepares, a person decides (Art. 22).
- **Reusable:** the same BRANE policies can be applied to other processes of the same company.

## Demo script (≈ 3–5 min)
1. Show the problem
2. Run the agent on a clean case (T1)
3. Show sensitive data being protected (T2) and the bypass attempt failing (T4)
4. Show the test protocol / logs
5. Business impact & next steps
